# Registers and dialects

Read this when the brief asks for a dialect, a "local" voice, or a brand tone that isn't plain product UI.

## The default: simplified MSA

Simplified Modern Standard Arabic (فصحى مبسطة) is grammatical MSA with everyday vocabulary and short sentences. It is understood in every Arabic-speaking country and is what users expect from product interfaces. Most leading apps in the region use it for UI.

Signs you've drifted too formal:

- Classical connectors: `إذ إنّ`، `حيث إنّه`، `علمًا بأنّ`.
- Ornate politeness: `يرجى التكرم`، `نحيطكم علمًا`.
- Rare vocabulary where a common word exists: `أوصِد` instead of `أغلق`.

Signs you've drifted too casual for UI:

- Dialect words in settings or errors: `وش`، `إيش`، `عايز`، `بدك`.
- English slang transliterated: `أوكي`، `سوري`.

## When dialect works

- Ads, social posts and campaign landing pages aimed at one country.
- Brand voice that is deliberately local (a Riyadh food delivery app, an Egyptian fintech).
- Chat-style onboarding or a mascot, when the brand already talks that way.

When it backfires:

- In a product used across several Arab countries. Saudi dialect reads as foreign in Morocco; Egyptian is widely understood but still marks the product as Egyptian.
- In errors, payments, security and legal text. Users want calm and precise there.
- When the writer isn't a native speaker of that dialect. Wrong dialect is worse than MSA.

## The same message in four voices

| English | Simplified MSA | Saudi / Gulf | Egyptian | Levantine |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome! | أهلًا بك! | هلا والله! | أهلًا بيك! | أهلين! |
| Your order is on its way | طلبك في الطريق | طلبك بالطريق | طلبك في السكة | طلبك عالطريق |
| Want 20% off? | هل تريد خصم 20%؟ | تبي خصم 20%؟ | عايز خصم 20%؟ | بدك خصم 20%؟ |
| Order now | اطلب الآن | اطلب الحين | اطلب دلوقتي | اطلب هلّق |
| We miss you | اشتقنا إليك | وحشتنا | وحشتنا | اشتقنالك |
| See you soon | نراك قريبًا | نشوفك قريب | نشوفك قريب | منشوفك قريبًا |
| Hurry, limited offer | أسرع، العرض لفترة محدودة | الحق، العرض لفترة محدودة | الحق، العرض لفترة محدودة | الحق حالك، العرض لفترة محدودة |
| What's new? | ما الجديد؟ | وش الجديد؟ | إيه الجديد؟ | شو الجديد؟ |

Notes:

- `وحشتنا` is used in both Gulf and Egyptian speech.
- Gulf dialect has a feminine `ـج`/`ـچ` in some areas (Kuwait, Iraq-adjacent). Don't use it unless the brief targets that area.
- Egyptian `بيك` is masculine; `بيكي` is feminine. Dialect makes gender neutrality harder. Use plural (`بيكم`، `عايزين`) or rephrase.

## Brand tone within MSA

MSA has its own range. Adjust these without leaving it:

| Lever | Warmer | Neutral | More formal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greeting | أهلًا {name} | مرحبًا {name} | مرحبًا بك |
| Confirmation | تم! كل شيء جاهز. | تم الحفظ. | تم حفظ التغييرات. |
| Error | عذرًا، حصل خلل بسيط. | حدث خطأ ما. | تعذّر إتمام العملية. |
| Request | ممكن تساعدنا بتقييم سريع؟ | قيّم تجربتك | نرجو تقييم تجربتك |

The warmer column borrows from spoken Arabic (`ممكن`، `حصل`). That is fine in marketing and onboarding for most Gulf brands. Keep it out of payments and security.
