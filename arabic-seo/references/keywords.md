# Arabic keyword research

Arabic search is several markets that share a script. Research per country, then write per register.

## What varies

**Dialect and register.** People often search in a mix of Modern Standard Arabic and their dialect. Examples of the same intent:

| Intent | MSA | Saudi / Gulf | Egyptian |
|---|---|---|---|
| How much is… | كم سعر | بكم / كم سعر | بكام |
| How do I… | كيف | كيف / وش الطريقة | إزاي / ازاي |
| What is… | ما هو | وش هو / ايش | إيه هو / ايه |
| I want | أريد | أبي / أبغى | عايز |
| Nearby | قريب مني | قريب مني | جنبي / قريب مني |

Treat these as examples, not a rule. Confirm with real query data for the market.

**Spelling variants.** People type fast and skip details:

- Hamza on alef: `أ` / `إ` / `آ` / `ا` (`أسعار` versus `اسعار`)
- Ta marbuta versus ha at the end: `ة` / `ه` (`مدرسة` versus `مدرسه`)
- Alef maqsura versus ya: `ى` / `ي` (`مستشفى` versus `مستشفي`)
- With or without the article `ال` (`سعر الذهب` versus `سعر ذهب`)
- Diacritics (tashkeel) are almost never typed.
- Loanwords spelled several ways (`ايفون` / `آيفون`, `اونلاين` / `أونلاين`).

Google matches many of these variants to each other, but not all of them, and not equally for every query. Do not assume either way. Search the main variants in the target country (use a VPN or Google's country settings) and compare the results. If the results differ, the variant is a separate keyword.

**Arabizi** (Arabic in Latin letters and digits: `3arabi`, `7abibi`, `mat3am`) is common in chat and social media, and less common in search. Check volume before targeting it. Do not put it in titles unless the data shows demand.

**English inside Arabic queries.** Product and tech searches often mix scripts: `سعر iPhone 16`, `كورس Python`. Keep brand and product names in their original script in titles and headings.

**Digits.** Queries arrive in both Western (2026) and Arabic-Indic (٢٠٢٦) digits. Gulf and Saudi users commonly type Western digits. Use one system in visible content per site, and keep machine-readable values in Western digits.

## Research steps

1. Pick the country first: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt and so on. Volumes and phrasing differ a lot between them.
2. Seed list: write the intent in MSA plus the target dialect, plus common spelling variants.
3. Pull volumes with location and language set to the target country and Arabic, for example in Google Keyword Planner. Treat third-party tool volumes for Arabic as rough. Many tools under-sample Arabic.
4. Check Google Trends with the country set, to compare variants and spot seasonality (Ramadan, Eid, back to school, White Friday / Black Friday, Saudi National Day on 23 September).
5. Read the live results for the top terms in that country: what page types rank, which dialect the titles use, and whether results are Arabic or English.
6. After launch, use Search Console's Performance report filtered by country and query to find the real phrasing that brings impressions, then fold it into headings and FAQs.

## Writing with the research

- One main keyword per page. Put it in the title, H1, first paragraph and slug (if you use Arabic slugs).
- Use dialect phrasing in FAQ questions and subheadings where it matches real queries. Keep body text in clear MSA unless the brand voice is dialect.
- Answer the question in the first paragraph. AI overviews and featured snippets quote short, direct answers.
