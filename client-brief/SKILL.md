# Client brief

Turn one messy client message into a brief a person can send back. Read only what they pasted. Leave gaps as questions.

## When to use

- A WhatsApp thread, email, or transcript needs to become a clear job description.
- The user asks "what are they actually asking for?"

## When not to use

- They want a price, a quote, or payment terms. Use freelance-quote for that, after this brief exists.
- The message is a contract to review for legal risk. Say so and stop.

## Workflow

1. Read the whole message before writing.
2. Fill `references/brief-template.md`. Copy a fact only when the message states it.
3. Put anything missing under Open questions. Do not guess the budget, the deadline, the audience, or the deliverable count.
4. Split the ask into In scope and Out of scope. Out of scope is only what they explicitly excluded, or what a reader could confuse with the ask.
5. End with one next step: the single question that unblocks the work.
6. Show the brief to the user. Do not send it to the client unless they ask.

## Rules

- Quote the client's words when a phrase is easy to misread. Keep the quote short.
- If two messages disagree, write both and ask which one stands.
- Arabic and English can sit in the same brief. Do not translate a requirement into a different requirement.
- No price, no discount, no timeline you made up.

## Done check

- Every line is either in the source or marked as an open question.
- In scope does not contain a deliverable they never mentioned.
- The next step is one question, not a plan.
