# Proposal template

Use when the client needs to be convinced, not just priced: competitive bids, new clients, larger budgets. A proposal wraps the quote in context. Keep it under five pages. Lead with their problem, not your bio.

---

# [PROJECT NAME]: proposal for [CLIENT]

[DATE] · Prepared by [YOUR NAME / STUDIO]

## 1. Where you are now

[Two or three sentences in the client's own words: the current situation and what is not working. Quote the brief or the call if you can.]

## 2. What success looks like

- [Measurable outcome, e.g. "Launch the bilingual site before the Ramadan campaign on [DATE]."]
- [Outcome 2]
- [Outcome 3]

## 3. Approach

[Short phases, each with what happens and what the client gets at the end.]

1. **Discovery** ([N] week): [what you do]. You get: [artifact].
2. **Design** ([N] weeks): [what you do]. You get: [artifact].
3. **Build** ([N] weeks): [what you do]. You get: [artifact].
4. **Launch and handover**: [what you do]. You get: [artifact].

| | Essential | Standard (recommended) | Extended |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Scope line] | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| [Scope line] | | ✓ | ✓ |
| [Scope line] | | | ✓ |
| Revision rounds | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Timeline | [N] weeks | [N] weeks | [N] weeks |
| **Investment** | [AMOUNT] | [AMOUNT] | [AMOUNT] |

Why Standard: [one sentence tied to their goal].

## 5. Timeline

[Phase table with dates and the client inputs each phase waits on.]

## 6. Why us

- [Relevant past project with a concrete result.]
- [Relevant skill or specialty, e.g. bilingual Arabic/English interfaces.]
- [How you work: weekly updates, one point of contact.]

## 7. Terms

Payment schedule, assumptions, exclusions, revisions, change requests, validity date. Copy them from the quote.

## 8. Next steps

1. Pick an option and reply to accept.
2. We send the agreement and the deposit invoice.
3. Kickoff on [DATE].
