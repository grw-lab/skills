# Discovery questions by project type

Pick only the questions whose answers change price, timeline, or risk. Five is usually enough.

## Always

- What is the deadline, and is it fixed (event, campaign, funding) or flexible?
- Who approves the work, and who gives feedback?
- What budget range do you have in mind?
- Who supplies content (copy, images, translations), and by when?

## Website

- How many pages or templates? Which are unique layouts and which repeat?
- Which languages? Is Arabic (right-to-left) needed at launch?
- CMS needed? Who edits content after launch?
- Integrations: forms, booking, payments, CRM, newsletter, analytics?
- Hosting and domain: existing or new? Who manages them?
- Is there an old site to migrate content or SEO redirects from?

## App or product

- Platforms: web, iOS, Android? Native or cross-platform?
- Auth: email, phone OTP, social, SSO?
- Admin panel or dashboard needed?
- Third-party APIs: are the docs and credentials ready?
- Expected users at launch, and data that must be kept private?

## Brand or design

- Number of concepts and rounds of revisions?
- Deliverables and file formats (Figma, SVG, print-ready PDF)?
- Usage rights and ownership of final files?
- Existing brand guidelines to follow?

## Content or copy

- Word count or number of pieces?
- Research and interviews included?
- Who approves tone and facts?
- Bilingual: translated or written natively in each language?

## Ongoing work

- Expected hours per month, and how predictable?
- Response time you need for urgent issues?
- Minimum term you are comfortable with?
