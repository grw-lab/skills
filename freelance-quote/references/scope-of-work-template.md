# Scope of work (SOW) template

Use after the client accepts, to lock down exactly what will be delivered. It is the document both sides check when there is a disagreement. Be specific: numbers, formats, counts.

---

# Scope of work: [PROJECT NAME]

**Client:** [NAME] · **Provider:** [NAME] · **Reference quote:** [Q-NUMBER] · **Date:** [DATE]

## 1. Objective

[One paragraph: the business goal this work serves.]
